## Mutation check (scratch copy of the database, dropped afterwards)

The schema makes "a second confirmed hold owning a booked seat" impossible to insert: a seat points to its hold through the single column `seats.hold_id` (there is no hold-seat table), so it can belong to only one hold. The closest mutations the schema allows:

- Insert a second CONFIRMED hold for the event (no seat linked): `one succeeded payment per confirmed hold` failed (`violating holds=1`); the other checks stayed green.
- Insert a second SUCCEEDED payment for a confirmed hold: refused by `payments_hold_id_key` (unique on `hold_id`), so it cannot be written.
- Add 1 cent to one ledger credit: `ledger balanced per payment` failed (`unbalanced payments=1`).
